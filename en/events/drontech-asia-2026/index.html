About the Event

Asia's Leading Drone Exhibition — 3rd Edition

DronTech Asia 2026, the region's premier drone technology exhibition, returns for its 3rd edition on 11–13 November 2026 at IMPACT Muang Thong Thani, Bangkok — building on the success of the previous edition with 100+ exhibitors from 15 countries, over 5,000 trade visitors and 5,500+ sqm of exhibition space.

INSEE UAV × Thai Sky Vision will be exhibiting the INSEE-250 and INSEE-350 E-VTOL Fixed-Wing UAVs, designed and built in Thailand, together with the full YellowScan LiDAR lineup for every mapping & survey mission — see the aircraft up close, talk to our team, and explore results from real missions at our booth.

Press Conference

Thai Sky Vision — Official Partner of DronTech Asia 2026

Mr. Sakun Ariyachotima, CEO of Thai Sky Vision / INSEE UAV, attended the official launch press conference of DronTech Asia 2026 at IMPACT Muang Thong Thani, where Thai Sky Vision was announced as an Official Partner of the exhibition and received the Handover Contract from organiser GML Exhibition, alongside leading partners in Thailand's drone industry.

This partnership reaffirms Thai Sky Vision / INSEE UAV's role as a leader in UAV and LiDAR survey technology in Thailand — visit our booth at the show, 11–13 November 2026.
