INSEE UAV at INTERGEO 2024

Bringing INSEE-250 + YellowScan LiDAR to the world stage at Europe's largest Geospatial Technology exhibition.

location_on Stuttgart, Germany
calendar_month 24–26 September 2024
groups Global Geospatial & Surveying Professionals
Event Recap

Bringing INSEE-250 + YellowScan LiDAR to the Global Geospatial Stage

INTERGEO 2024, held in Stuttgart, Germany, is Europe's largest Geospatial Technology exhibition — a global meeting point for industry experts. INSEE UAV was proud to participate, presenting our flagship INSEE-250 UAV — a VTOL Fixed-Wing platform — alongside the advanced YellowScan LiDAR system, giving international attendees the opportunity to experience the platform first-hand.

Over three days, the INSEE UAV team demonstrated our VTOL design and long-endurance flight, showcased real LiDAR point cloud outputs, and walked attendees through INSEE Pilot software and Ground Station — developed in-house in Thailand. We received strong interest from survey companies, research institutions, and operators across Forestry, Construction, and Mining in Europe and globally.
