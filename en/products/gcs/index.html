INSEE Pilot Software

Engineered in Thailand

Our In-house Flight Control Software

INSEE Pilot is the flight control software developed in-house by the INSEE UAV team in Thailand — purpose-built for real-world missions, intuitive to operate, from Mission Planning to In-Flight Monitoring on INSEE-GCS-01.

map

Mission Planning — Polygon area, Waypoint, Grid, Corridor — supports every mapping workflow

monitoring

Real-time HUD — Roll, Pitch, Altitude, Speed, Throttle, Wind, Range Finder — all on a single screen

flight

Flight Modes — Auto, Loiter, Stabilize, QuadPlane VTOL — switch modes instantly

radar

Air Traffic Awareness — displays nearby manned aircraft on the map in real time (via Transponder Receiver) — safer operations in shared airspace

checklist

Pre-flight Checklist — comprehensive system check before every flight to reduce risk

support

Local Support — fixes, improvements and updates handled by our in-country team — no waiting for foreign vendors
