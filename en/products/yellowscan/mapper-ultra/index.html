YellowScan
Mapper
Ultra.
Go Further. See Through. Map Faster.

YellowScan Mapper Ultra — a high-performance LiDAR system for long-range aerial surveys. Built for high-altitude flights with wide-area coverage in a single sortie. Its Multi-Echo 7 returns technology penetrates forest canopy for crisp ground data. Ideal for Corridor Mapping and mountainous forested areas where performance matters most.

Technologies inside SBG Quanta Micro YellowScan
Key Differentiators
🌲

7 Echoes Per Shot
See Through Vegetation

Multi-echo technology records up to 7 returns per shot — cleanly penetrating forest canopy to extract ground data beneath dense vegetation, ideal for forestry and heavily covered terrain.

my_location

Long Range
640m Laser Reach

Maximum range of 640 m supports flights up to 240 m AGL — higher altitude means wider single-sortie coverage and fewer flight lines for corridor and large-area work.

bolt

Fast Coverage
3.5M Points/sec

Fires at 500,000 shots/sec, producing up to 3.5 million points per second — covering more ground faster and cutting flight time.

flight

Multi-Platform
Built for Any UAV

Compatible with multi-rotor, single-rotor, VTOL fixed-wing and fixed-wing UAVs — 1,950 g and platform-agnostic, ideally paired with INSEE-350 for Long-Range Missions.
