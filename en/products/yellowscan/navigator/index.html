BATHYMETRIC GREEN LASER 532nm Topographic + Underwater
YellowScan
YellowScan
Navigator.
Bathymetric & topographic LiDAR solution for UAVs

YellowScan Navigator is a Bathymetric UAV LiDAR system that surveys above and below the water surface at the same time using a 532 nm green laser that penetrates the water surface — ideal for coastal, river, and reservoir surveys, and shallow waters, delivering above- and below-water topography in a single sortie.

Technologies inside SBG Systems YellowScan
Key Differentiators

Water Mapping

Surveys land and submerged surfaces simultaneously in a single sortie, with a 532 nm Green Laser that penetrates the water surface — cutting time and cost across coastal, river, and reservoir surveys.
