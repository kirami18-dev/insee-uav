RGB CAMERA PLUG & PLAY Colorize Point Cloud
YellowScan
Camera
Modules.
LiDAR and photogrammetry combined

RGB cameras for YellowScan LiDAR systems — capture LiDAR data and color imagery in a single flight. Generate Colorized Point Cloud or Orthophoto instantly with CloudStation. Three models available: Single 26MP, Single 61MP and Dual 26MP — swappable across multiple LiDAR systems.

Designed by YellowScan
Why Add a Camera?
📷

Triggered by
Your LiDAR

The camera is triggered automatically by the LiDAR system — precisely synced with LiDAR data, no extra setup, capturing imagery exactly where you scan.

🎯

Calibrated with
Your LiDAR

The camera is factory-calibrated with the LiDAR — delivering accurate, well-aligned Colorized Point Clouds with no recalibration required.

⚡

Powered by
the System

Powered directly by the LiDAR system — no extra battery required, reducing weight and setup complexity so you can fly right away.

📱

Easy to Use
Plug & Play

Easy to mount and swap between different LiDAR models — Swappable Design. Process in CloudStation to generate Orthophoto or Colorize Point Cloud instantly.
